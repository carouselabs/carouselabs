CarouseLabs Engage for X is installed

One quick step first, so it's always within reach.

  1. Click the puzzle-piece icon in Chrome's toolbar, to the right of the address bar.
  2. Find CarouseLabs Engage for X in the list and click the pin icon next to it.
  3. The icon now stays in your toolbar. Click it any time to open the side panel.
Open X

Then sign in

Open the side panel from the toolbar icon and sign in with your CarouseLabs account. The panel picks up your sign-in on its own, with no refresh needed.

Then click Reply on any post on X, or open a DM, and the panel writes it in your voice. You read it and press Reply or Send yourself.
