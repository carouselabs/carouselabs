CarouseLabs Comment is installed

One quick step first, so it's always within reach.

  1. Click the puzzle-piece icon in Chrome's toolbar, to the right of the address bar.
  2. Find CarouseLabs Comment in the list and click the pin icon next to it.
  3. The icon now stays in your toolbar. Click it any time to open the side panel.
Open LinkedIn

Open the side panel from the toolbar icon and sign in with your CarouseLabs account to get started. The panel will pick up your sign-in on its own, with no refresh needed. The keyboard shortcut Alt + Shift + G generates a comment once a post is selected.
