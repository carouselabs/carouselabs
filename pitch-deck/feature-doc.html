1Overview

CarouseLabs is an AI content-creation studio that turns a topic — or nothing at all — into a ready-to-post caption, on-brand image, or full multi-slide carousel, tailored to any of 8 supported platforms. It's built for founders, creators, and marketers who want consistent, high-quality social content without a design tool, a writer, or hours per post.

Every generation runs through the same underlying pipeline regardless of entry point: a caption is drafted first, and — for image or carousel formats — a 3-stage AI process plans the layout, writes a visual brief, and renders the final image. The product is built around two symmetric entry points (own idea vs. AI-suggested idea, see Section 2), three output formats (Section 3), and a personalization layer (Section 10) that shapes every generation to the user's profile, industry, and voice.

2Two Ways to Create

Every session starts from one of two entry points, both landing on the same breakdown → format-picker → generation flow.

2.1 Work on Own Idea

  • Title — up to 15 words, enforced client- and server-side.
  • Notes — "Tell us more about your idea," up to 650 words.
  • Claude (claude-sonnet-4-5) runs a Research Expansion pass that turns the title + notes into a full breakdown: an 800–2,000 word knowledge document, a refined hook, key talking points, and a strong ending line — generated immediately, with no separate "breakdown" step.

2.2 Trending / AI-Suggested Ideas

  • The dashboard generates 10 ideas per request, on demand — not a fixed daily batch. Mix: 3 Latest News + 3 Trending Topic + 3 Industry Topic + 1 Random-but-relevant.
  • "Latest News" ideas are grounded in real headlines from NewsAPI.org (last 48 hours) when available; otherwise Claude falls back to its own knowledge, labeled "[Trending]".
  • Rate-limited to 10 generations per hour per user.
  • "Load More" fetches 10 more, deduplicated against already-seen hooks, capped at 30 ideas per session.
  • "Work on Own Idea" is always pinned first in the feed, above the AI-suggested ideas.
  • Any idea can be duplicated into a fresh session (clones the idea + latest breakdown) without disturbing the original.

4The 3-Stage AI Pipeline

Image and carousel generation don't rely on a single prompt — they run through a 3-stage pipeline that mirrors a real design process.

  • Stage 1 — Plan the layout. GPT-4o decides how the content should be visually organized: for a single image, a layout approach (e.g. "Hero + Infographic"); for a carousel, the slide count, order, and section roles (Hook → Problem → Solution → CTA, etc).
  • Stage 2 — Write the visual brief. GPT-4o (primary) turns that structural decision, plus the caption and breakdown, into the actual image-generation prompt — a headline and detailed visual brief per slide for carousels. Falls back automatically to Claude Sonnet 4.5 on refusal or API error.
  • Stage 3 — Generate the image. OpenAI's gpt-image-2 renders the final visual — images.generate normally, or images.edit when a style reference is attached. Carousel slides are generated sequentially (not in parallel) to stay within rate limits.

This pipeline runs for every idea — own or AI-suggested — not just the Own Idea flow.

7Reference Image Style Matching

Upload one example post, and every generated visual matches its look — without copying it.

  • The reference image is sent to vision-capable models at up to three separate pipeline stages, each paired with an explicit instruction to extract style only — typography, color relationships, layout philosophy — and never copy layout, composition, icons, text, logos, or any identifiable visual element.
  • A "reference-only" structure mode lets a reference image drive composition/reading order specifically, independent of color or typography.
  • At the final image-generation stage, the reference is passed again to gpt-image-2 via images.edit, reinforcing "keep the tone and accents consistent; don't copy the content."
  • Uploaded references are client-side compressed (max 1024px, JPEG quality 0.8) before upload, to stay under payload limits.

8Regeneration & Editing Controls

Every generation surface (caption, image, carousel) shares a common set of fine-grained controls.

  • Tone Selector — 5 tones (Analytical, Candid, Playful, Authoritative, Story-driven) for the initial caption pass.
  • Custom Instructions — an optional free-text box (up to 1,000 characters) attached to any regeneration, e.g. "make it shorter," "add more stats."
  • Apply My Voice Guidelines — a checkbox that folds the user's saved writing-style guidelines into the regeneration (only shown if guidelines exist).
  • Hook Variations — AI-suggested alternate opening lines; clicking one swaps the caption's hook.
  • Hashtag Chips — suggested hashtags, click to append to the caption.
  • Version History — up to 2 prior versions kept and individually restorable, for both captions and carousels.
  • Image aspect ratio — 4:5 Portrait or 1:1 Square, remembered across sessions.
  • Per-slide regeneration — each carousel slide can be regenerated independently, with its own custom-instructions box, for 8 credits.
  • Download All as PDF — exports a full carousel as a print-ready PDF, one slide per page at native resolution.
Regeneration is capped at 2 uses per idea, per session — applied consistently across caption, image, and carousel regeneration.

9History, Pinned & Drafts

History

  • Search by keyword; filter by Caption / Image / Carousel
  • Idea-category tag (News / Trending / Industry / Random)
  • "Draft saved" badge when a session has unpublished work
  • Per-card: pin, duplicate, resume, or delete
  • "Continue" resumes exactly where a session left off

Pinned ("Content Wishlist")

  • Ideas saved at the idea level, before generation starts
  • No session is created just by pinning
  • Searchable, individually removable

Drafts

There's no separate drafts page — every generated post is saved with a DRAFT status by default, surfaced back in History via the "Draft saved" badge. A "Save Draft" button appears in every editor, and caption edits are additionally auto-saved in the background as a safety net.

10Onboarding & Personalization

A one-time profile (editable anytime in Settings → Profile) personalizes every idea and caption generated.

  • Identity — one role: Founder/Entrepreneur, Content Creator, Freelancer, Operator/Manager, Consultant, Executive/CXO, or Other.
  • Industry & Niche — searchable industry, plus a free-text niche description (up to 500 words).
  • Content Topics — 3 to 7 topic tags, from suggestions or free text.
  • Target Audience — audience role, seniority (6 levels), audience industry, and core problem being solved (up to 200 words).
  • Content Goals — multi-select from 5 goals (brand-building, lead gen, audience growth, thought leadership, education), with one marked primary.
  • Tone & Voice — multi-select from 5 base tones (Professional, Conversational, Inspirational, Educational, Bold).

This profile feeds directly into idea generation (topic selection, news-query targeting) and every caption prompt (audience, goals, tone) — it's functional personalization, not a cosmetic form.

11Voice Presets & Voice Guidelines

  • Voice Presets — named, reusable combinations of the 5 base tones. Up to 5 presets can be saved, added, edited, or deleted.
  • Voice Guidelines — a single free-form text block (soft target of ~1,000 words) where a user pastes tone/phrasing instructions or example best-performing posts, for the AI to match their exact writing style. Applied via the "Apply my voice guidelines" checkbox at generation time.

12LinkedIn Direct Posting

  • Connect a LinkedIn account once via OAuth (Settings → Account); the connection status, name, and any expiry are shown there.
  • "Post to LinkedIn" is available on every generation surface — caption-only, single image, and carousel — and posts the caption plus up to 8 images directly to the connected account.
  • On success, a confirmation links straight to the live post.
  • This is currently the only platform with direct publishing; all others are copy-paste.

Credit top-ups

Paid-plan users can buy extra credits anytime: $2 per 100 credits, in multiples of 100 (min 100 / max 5,000 per purchase). Top-up credits expire 2 months after purchase and are only spent after the monthly allowance runs out. Checkout runs through a Lemon Squeezy overlay; plan changes and cancellations route through the same billing portal to avoid double charges.

14Account & Billing

  • Billing tab — current plan, renewal/cancellation date, a credits-remaining progress bar (with a low-credit warning at ≤100 left), the top-up widget, upgrade/manage actions, and a full plan-comparison grid.
  • Account tab — account email and plan badge, LinkedIn connect/disconnect, and Export My Data — a one-click JSON download of every idea and post ever generated.
  • Upgrading Pro → Growth is scheduled for the next renewal (no immediate charge); all other plan and cancellation changes go through the Lemon Squeezy customer portal.

15Admin Tools

An internal console, visible only to admin accounts, for operating the content product.

  • Overview dashboard — user counts by plan, total posts/carousels/images generated, credits used, and estimated API cost; quick actions to grant credits or change a user's plan by email.
  • User management — searchable table plus per-user detail pages, bulk plan/suspend actions.
  • Credit administration, all-user post browsing, and a subscriptions overview with CSV export.
  • Analytics — usage charts and cohort retention.
  • Audit log of every admin action, and a broadcast composer for sending email announcements to users.

16Additional Features

  • Transactional email system (via Resend) — welcome, onboarding-complete, first-post celebration, upgrade confirmation, low/exhausted credits, top-up receipt, monthly reset, renewal reminder, and cancellation confirmation.
  • Contact form — routes to support@carouselabs.com, rate-limited to 3 messages/hour per sender.
  • Data export — full account export as JSON (see Section 14).
  • PDF export — full carousel export as a print-ready PDF (see Section 8).
  • Loading mini-game — a short "stop the needle" timing game shown while content generates, so the wait doesn't feel idle; a rare win quietly notifies the team to follow up with the player.
